# SupplyFlow User Feedback Summary

This document contains a structured summary of the feedback collected from our 12 pilot users during the testing phase. All data is sourced directly from our live user feedback tracking sheet.

## Overall Metrics
- **Total Users Surveyed**: 12
- **Average Rating**: 4.16 / 5.0
- **Overall Sentiment**: Highly positive, with strong praise for transparency and speed. Most feature requests focused on reporting, QoL (Quality of Life) enhancements, and expanded integrations.
